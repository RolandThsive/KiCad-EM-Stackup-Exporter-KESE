Research, education, personal study and use by noncommercial organizations
(schools, universities, public research bodies, charities, government) are
allowed under those terms at no cost.

Any commercial use, including use inside a for-profit company, in a paid
product or service, or in work done for a paying client, needs a separate
written commercial license from the copyright holder, RolandThsive.

## What counts as commercial use

The [LICENSE](LICENSE) alone decides what is allowed. The examples below are
how the copyright holder reads it, so you can tell in advance which side you
are on.

Needs a commercial license, for example:

- Running KESE at, or for, a for-profit company, including for internal
  design, evaluation or simulation work.
- Using KESE to design, model, simulate or document a product or part that is
  sold, or meant to be sold.
- Using KESE in paid consulting, contract or client work.
- Selling KESE, a modified version of it, or a product or service built on it
  or on its output files.

Free under the license:

- Research and teaching at schools, universities and public research bodies.
- Personal study, hobby projects and private research with no commercial
  application in view.
- Use by charities and government institutions.

If you are not sure, ask before you use KESE.

## Getting a commercial license

To ask for one, open an issue on this repository or contact
[RolandThsive](https://github.com/RolandThsive) on GitHub.
