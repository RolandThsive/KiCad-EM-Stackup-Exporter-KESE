# Feature Test Boards

One small board per group of KiCad features that KESE reads. Each board is made by
`Generator/tests/feature_boards/make.py`; change a board there and run it again rather than
editing these files by hand:

```bash
cd Generator
python tests/feature_boards/make.py
```

The regression suite (`Generator/tests/test_boards.py`) runs every board here through every
output; after changing a board, run `python tests/test_boards.py --accept` to record it again.

Boards in KiCad 7's format were read back, zone-filled and saved by KiCad 7 itself, so they are
exactly what KiCad writes, including the outlines of TrueType text. Features that only KiCad 8, 9
or 10 have are on hand-written boards in that version's format, written from KiCad's own file
writer source; open those once in that KiCad to confirm they load.

## Example Boards

The three example boards of `Generator/Input/` are here too, so that the regression suite runs
them with the boards above. Each is a whole board that uses many of these features at once.
`make.py` does not make them, and does not write this section when it rewrites this README.

| File | Format | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `21_rf_module.kicad_pcb` | KiCad 7 (saved by KiCad 7) | A 4-layer RF sensor module with a plated edge (edge_plating yes): a GND border on every copper layer along the rounded outline and around a cut-out, the mask open over it, and a fence of GND vias in it. A QFN-32 microcontroller with its crystal, a 6-pad SWD connector and a SOIC-8 flash; a QFN-20 radio on an SPI bus, its 50 ohm line through a matching network to a coax connector between rows of stitching vias; an LDO fed from a 6-pin header, three LEDs, test points on both sides, GND planes on F.Cu, In1.Cu and B.Cu, In2.Cu split into +3V3 and +5V, plated mounting holes, silkscreen on both sides, and the KESE logo of docs/logo on the back in bare copper, copper under the mask and silkscreen. Blue mask, white silk, ENIG. |
| `22_hdi_module.kicad_pcb` | KiCad 7 (saved by KiCad 7) | A 6-layer HDI radio module, 1+4+1: microvias from each outer layer to the layer under it, buried vias from In1.Cu to In4.Cu, blind vias from an outer layer to In2.Cu or In3.Cu, and through vias, on a stack-up of named glass styles with an LPI mask and dielectric constraints. A 64-ball BGA with a microvia in the pad of each inner ball and its supplies on staggered buried vias, fanned out on In1.Cu to 43 castellated pads (castellated_pads yes) on three sides; a USON-8 flash, a 2016 crystal, a buck converter making +1V1, and a matching network feeding a meander antenna in a keep-out, its line referenced to In2.Cu through a cut in In1.Cu. | `23_pcie_card.kicad_pcb` | KiCad 7 (saved by KiCad 7) | A 4-layer PCIe x1 gigabit Ethernet card that powers its port (PoE), within a low-profile card's size: gold fingers on both sides at a 1.00 mm pitch on a bevelled tongue (edge_connector bevelled), PRSNT1# and PRSNT2# 1 mm shorter and joined by a single trace, no plane under the fingers and a bulk capacitor at them for each supply (PCI Express CEM Rev 2.0). GND on F.Cu, In1.Cu and B.Cu, +3V3 and a +1V0 island on In2.Cu, the KESE logo on the front. Where finger B1 sits from the bracket and the outline next to the bracket are estimates. Green mask, white silk, hard gold. |
